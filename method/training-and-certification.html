Primary consulting method

Root to Crown

A comprehensive operating system for founder independence, succession, governance, leadership development, and knowledge transfer. Root to Crown helps you build an organization that can endure beyond any single leader — including you.

01

Roots

The foundational layer — values, purpose, founder's original intent, core identity, and the unwritten rules that shape culture.

02

Trunk

The structural core — governance, decision rights, roles, and the operating model that carries the organization's identity into the future.

03

Branches

The extending capabilities — leadership development, knowledge transfer, and the spread of institutional wisdom across teams and functions.

04

Crown

The outward expression and legacy — reputation, market position, stakeholder trust, and the organization's enduring impact.
