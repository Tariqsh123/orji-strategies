About the publication

Fair hearings, credible investigations, defensible decisions.

The Small Room is a standalone publication — not a consulting offering. It brings together Michael Orji's legal training, investigation experience, and procedural-fairness insight into a single, structured framework for fair hearings, workplace investigations, due process, credibility assessment, and defensible decision-making.

The book is designed for practitioners and leaders who want to apply its framework themselves. For consulting engagements on these topics, see HEARTH™, our human-centered workplace investigation and procedural-fairness method.

About the author, Michael Orji
Inside the book

What the publication covers

The Small Room walks through each layer of a fair, defensible process — from investigation planning to final decisions.

A framework for reviewing investigation practices — how to assess existing practices, identify gaps, and improve them.
Investigation planning — how to plan investigations that are thorough, fair, and defensible from start to finish.
Procedural fairness and due process — how to evaluate processes against best practices for fairness and due process.
Credibility assessment — a structured approach for assessing credibility effectively and defensibly.
Hearing and decision-making process — hearing procedures, decision-making frameworks, and documentation quality.
Investigator and decision-maker guidance — skills for conducting fair, thorough investigations and hearings.
Organizational trust — how processes affect trust in leadership and institutional confidence.
Policy and process evaluation — aligning policies with procedural fairness and legal best practices.

About the author

Written by Michael Orji, JD, Th.D.

Michael Orji brings together legal training, investigation experience, business management, mediation work, and theological depth. The Small Room reflects his conviction that organizations endure when their decisions are fair, their processes are defensible, and their people are treated with dignity.

Michael is also the founder of Orji Strategies, where Root to Crown is the primary consulting method and HEARTH™ is the human-centered workplace investigation and procedural-fairness method.
